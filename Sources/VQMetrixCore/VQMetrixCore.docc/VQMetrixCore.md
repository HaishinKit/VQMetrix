# ``VQMetrixCore``

Measure video compression quality with HaishinKit and libvmaf.

VQMetrix runs local macOS benchmarks and produces per-frame, per-setting results.
Android creates exact reference frames and multiple encoded candidates for evaluation
on the Mac. The Android-to-Mac receiver is a separately specified next integration;
local macOS measurement and Android material generation are available now.
